Where students go

Study destinations we place students into every intake

Each country suits a different budget, timeline and career goal. Your counsellor will tell you honestly if a country isn't the right fit for your profile. See how we take you from shortlist to visa in our five-stage process, or check the test prep classes most destinations require.

Australia

Post-study work rights up to four years and the largest number of scholarship-linked pathways we place students into.

United Kingdom

One-year master's degrees mean lower total cost. The Graduate Route gives two years of post-study work.

Canada

Clear permanent residency pathway through the Express Entry system after a recognised post-graduate diploma.

United States

The widest range of research funding and assistantships for students who qualify for competitive programs.

New Zealand

Smaller class sizes and a faster visa turnaround than most destinations, usually six to eight weeks.

Japan

MEXT and university scholarships can cover full tuition for students with strong Japanese or English test scores.

Finland

Finland is a safe and innovative European country, offering high-quality education, modern facilities, and excellent opportunities for international students.

South Korea

Growing number of English-taught engineering and business programs with on-campus part-time work options.

Sweden

Sweden is a modern and innovative European country offering world-class education, excellent career opportunities, and a high quality of life for international students.

Denmark

A strong choice for international students, offering high-quality education, innovative programs, and a practical learning approach, with a welcoming environment and excellent career prospects.

The route, step by step

Five stages, one counsellor the whole way

Every student's file changes hands too many times at most agencies. Here, the person you meet in stage one signs off on your visa file in stage four. Read what students who've been through it say in our testimonials.

01

Free consultation

We map your academic record, budget and target intake against realistic country and course options — no pressure to pick something today.

02

Shortlist and apply

You get three to five matched universities, not thirty. We prepare your SOP, transcripts and references and submit every application ourselves.

03

Offer and funding

We compare offer letters side by side, apply for scholarships you qualify for, and help arrange an education loan if you need one.

04

Visa file

A former visa officer on our team reviews your documents, runs a mock interview, and lodges the file with you present, not on your behalf alone.

05

Pre-departure

Flights, accommodation, airport pickup and an orientation session on banking, tenancy and part-time work rules before you leave Kathmandu.
